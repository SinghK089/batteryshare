How to unlock it

  1. Join the BatteryShare Testers group (tap Join group). Use the same Google account as the Play Store on your phone.
  2. Accept the test invite, then install BatteryShare from Google Play. If it says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.
  3. Got a Wear OS watch? Also accept the watch face test and install BatteryShare Rings Watch Face. The BatteryShare watch app installs from the phone test.
  4. Open BatteryShare and sign in with Google.
  5. Come back here on your phone and tap Open in BatteryShare. Pro unlocks right away.

Open in BatteryShare

Or in the app go to Settings → Redeem a code and enter the code above.

Pro access lasts for the number of days set for your invite, starting when you redeem it. You'll see the end date in Settings.
