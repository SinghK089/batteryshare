BatteryShare

All your batteries, on every device.

BatteryShare shows the battery of your phone, watch, tablet and Bluetooth accessories on each of them, with alerts when something runs low and Find My Device to ring a misplaced one.

Apps

  • BatteryShare for Android phones and tablets, with home-screen widgets
  • BatteryShare for Wear OS, with tiles and complications
  • The BatteryShare Rings watch face
