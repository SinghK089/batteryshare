BatteryShare

Privacy policy

Effective 11 October 2026. Applies to the BatteryShare apps for Android and Wear OS and the BatteryShare Rings watch face (together, "BatteryShare").

In short: BatteryShare only handles battery information about your own devices, so they can show it to each other. It doesn't use your location, contacts, files or messages, has no ads or analytics, and never sells or shares your data. You sign in with Google to use the app, and you can delete your account at any time.

How it is shared

Between your phone and watch, BatteryShare uses Bluetooth when they're near each other and Google's Wear OS Data Layer service (encrypted) when they're apart. This data stays within your own devices.

Cloud sync. You sign in with Google to use BatteryShare. The data above is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) under your account, so your other devices, such as a tablet, can see it. Security rules let only your account read or change it. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by Google. Google's processing is covered by the Firebase privacy information.

BatteryShare does not sell data, share it with third parties, use it for advertising, or include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Watch face

The BatteryShare watch face contains no code and collect no data. They only display information on your watch, including complication data you choose (from BatteryShare or other apps), which never leaves the watch through the face.

Chrome extension

The BatteryShare extension for Chrome (Windows, Mac, Chromebook) reads only this computer's battery level, charging state and estimated time left, and the name you give the computer. After you sign in (with Google, or with email and password), it stores these in your BatteryShare account so your other devices can show them, and reads your other devices' battery, your profile name and avatar, and your Pro status to show them in the toolbar.

To show insights (time left, drain against usual, charging history) it keeps up to a week of battery readings for your devices on this computer only. Alerts and notifications are created on this computer and kept for 7 days. For Find My Device it reads and writes ring requests in your account, the same way the app does, and plays a sound when you ring this computer. You can switch ringing off in Settings. It doesn't read your browsing, tabs or any website content.

Permissions

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth connect): to read the battery of Bluetooth devices already paired with your phone, using the standard Bluetooth Battery Service. BatteryShare doesn't scan for devices or use location.
  • Notifications: battery alerts and reports, and the Find My Device ring.
  • Calendar (optional): only when you turn on reminders before calendar events. BatteryShare reads the start time, title and place of events in the next day on your phone, and nothing else.
  • Run at startup: so morning, alarm and weekly reminders keep working after a restart.
  • Vibration, wake lock, audio settings and a short foreground service: to ring loudly and briefly (about 45 seconds) when you use Find My Device.

Keeping and deleting your data

Battery data on your devices is replaced as new readings arrive. Battery history on your phone keeps about a week of readings and two months of charging sessions, and is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app. Cloud data is kept until you delete it. You can delete your account and all cloud data in the app (Settings → Cloud sync → Delete account) or by following the steps on the account deletion page. Signing out stops syncing but keeps your cloud data until you delete it.

Children

BatteryShare is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
